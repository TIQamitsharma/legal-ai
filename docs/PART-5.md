# PART 5 — Showcase gallery (pre-defined best-use prompts)

Keep PARTS 1–4 unchanged. Build `/showcase`: a curated grid of **one-click demo cards**. Each card shows a title, the feature it demonstrates (badge: CLO / Skill / Workflow / Research / Drafting / Review), a 1-line "what to watch for", and a "Run" button that opens the right page with the prompt and inputs pre-filled. Group into 4 rows. In Demo Mode each card returns its pre-built output instantly.

**Row 1 — "The virtual legal department"**
1. **Ask the CLO — triage a messy matter** (CLO): "Our client, a Manchester software house, delivered a bespoke platform to a retailer 10 months ago. The retailer refuses to pay the final £120,000 instalment, claims the system was defective, and has just hired our client's lead developer. The contract has an England & Wales governing law clause and a £50,000 liability cap. What do we do?" — *Watch: staffing across Contracts, Litigation and Employment; limitation and pre-action flags; recommended workflow.*
2. **Jurisdiction guardrail** (CLO): "A Glasgow-based contractor wants to issue a claim against an Edinburgh client for unpaid invoices of £30,000. Prepare a Letter Before Claim under the Pre-Action Protocol." — *Watch: UK Counsel flags Scots law; refuses to apply England & Wales procedure.*

**Row 2 — "Contracts and drafting"**
3. **Review a one-sided SaaS contract** (Review, Sample A): acting for the customer. — *Watch: UCTA s.2(1) negligence exclusion, £500 cap, penalty clause analysed under Cavendish v Makdessi, New York jurisdiction mismatch.*
4. **Redline and negotiation playbook** (Skill: redline-proposer → negotiation-position-planner, Sample A). — *Watch: opening / fallback / walk-away for each point.*
5. **Part 36 offer** (Drafting): Defendant offers £45,000 to settle the whole of the claimant's claim, taking into account the counterclaim, relevant period 21 days. — *Watch: mandatory Part 36 content included.*
6. **Letter Before Claim — debt** (Drafting): Calder Joinery Ltd v Marlow Developments Ltd, £86,400 unpaid invoices plus statutory late-payment interest. — *Watch: Practice Direction steps, statutory interest under the 1998 Act, ADR offer.*

**Row 3 — "Disputes and advocacy"**
7. **Dispute viability in 10 minutes** (★ Workflow 10, demo matter: Kingsmead Software). — *Watch: Red/Amber/Green scorecard, human gate, sue/settle recommendation.*
8. **Limitation check** (Skill): breach of a simple contract on 3 March 2020, latent defect discovered 2024, claimant is a company. — *Watch: 6-year period for simple contract, flags whether any extension may apply and what needs verifying.*
9. **Cross-examination plan** (Skill): defendant's witness statement says goods were delivered on 12 May; delivery note is dated 19 May and signed by a different employee. — *Watch: issue-led, ethical questioning plan.*
10. **Attack my own submissions** (Skill: adversarial-reviewer): paste a 1-page skeleton argument. — *Watch: opposing counsel's best points and weak authorities flagged.*

**Row 4 — "Compliance, investigations and live research"**
11. **Ransomware — 72-hour clock** (★ Workflow 5, Hollins & Webb demo). — *Watch: countdown, ICO notification analysis, draft notification.*
12. **Grounded research** (Research): "What is the test for whether a liquidated damages clause is an unenforceable penalty under English law?" — sources: Find Case Law + legislation.gov.uk. — *Watch: every proposition linked to a retrieved official source; gaps flagged instead of guessed.*
13. **Company due-diligence snapshot** (Research company check): "Pemberton Kitchens Ltd" (demo) — *Watch: Companies House + GLEIF data pulled live; officers, PSCs, charges.*
14. **Citation integrity check** (Research): paste a paragraph with two real citations and one fabricated one. — *Watch: the fabricated citation is flagged "Not found".*
15. **Contract review to full workflow** (★ Workflow 1, Sample A). — *Watch: seven stages, human gate before redlines, consolidated work-product export.*

Also add a floating **"Guided demo"** button that walks a presenter through cards 1 → 3 → 7 → 11 → 12 in a 15-minute sequence, with one presenter note per step.
